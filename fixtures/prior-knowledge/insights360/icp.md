# Insights360 — ideal customer profile (sample prior knowledge for the offline suite)

Product-level baseline, to be verified per campaign with fresh sources:

- Contact centres of 20 to 200 seats whose calls are recorded already and whose QA is a manual sample today.
- Sectors seen so far: insurance claims handling, debt collection, utilities, telecoms resellers.
- The buyer is usually the operations or quality lead; the sponsor is the head of customer service.
- A common trigger is a regulator or a large customer asking for evidence of how calls were handled.
