## Positioning frames (product-level, baseline)

How Insights360 is positioned against the competitive set, drawn from
the website and product. These are the durable framings the campaign team can use
across campaigns; sector-specific positioning is the research team's per-campaign
job.

**Explicit (named on the website):**

- **vs Manual QA processes** — Insights360 covers 100% of calls; manual
  sampling covers 2–5%.
- **"From reactive monitoring to proactive oversight"** — used on the
  compliance page. Frames Insights360 against the after-the-fact
  pattern of manual review. The most quotable positioning frame on
  the site — anchor compliance-led campaigns on this line.

**Implicit (positioning that the pricing and packaging do the work
of):**

- **vs Enterprise tools** (NICE, Verint, Calabrio, Qualtrics) — same
  coverage, no 50-seat minimum, ~10× cheaper per seat. Buyer can
  start at 5 seats.
- **vs Mid-market pure-play QA** (EvaluAgent, Scorebuddy) — competitive
  on capability; differentiated on transparent pricing, no annual
  lock-in, no 25-seat economic floor.
- **vs Full CCaaS platforms** (Talkdesk, Genesys, Five9) — Insights360
  can be layered on existing telephony; CCaaS requires platform
  migration. ~10–16× cheaper if the buyer doesn't need the full
  platform.
- **vs Marketing-attribution call analytics** (Infinity, Mediahawk,
  ResponseTap) — different category. Insights360 is QA-first, not
  marketing-attribution-first. Refer those buyers elsewhere.
- **vs Managed services / integrators** (Opus) — Insights360 is
  self-serve SaaS, not a consultancy engagement.

**Channel-of-distribution caveat:** Insights360 is bundled with C360
Plus and Premium. For prospects already on C360, the competitive
question is "do you need Insights360 as an add-on?" not "Insights360
vs alternative."

## Tier 1: Enterprise (out of reach for SMB legal buyers)

### NICE CXone
- **URL:** https://www.nice.com
- **Pricing (UK, G-Cloud 14):** £36–£109/agent/month depending on bundle; 400-agent minimum, 24-month commitment
- **Positioning:** Large enterprise, government, healthcare, financial services. QM + WFM integrated. AI (Enlighten) embedded.
- **Legal SMB relevance:** None. Minimum contract makes the segment commercially invisible to them.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

### Verint / Calabrio (combined since Nov 2025)
- **URL:** https://www.verint.com / https://www.calabrio.com
- **Pricing:** Not public. Enterprise POA. Calabrio positioned as "mid-market nimble"; Verint as large enterprise. Combined company post-Thoma Bravo acquisition integrating both portfolios.
- **Positioning:** Calabrio for 200–2,000 agents; Verint for large enterprise. No legal vertical named.
- **Legal SMB relevance:** None. Absorbed by integration work post-merger; no SMB motion evident.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

### Qualtrics XM Discover (formerly Clarabridge)
- **URL:** https://www.qualtrics.com
- **Pricing:** POA. Vendr median $28,533/year; range $6,525–$126,000.
- **Positioning:** Enterprise experience management suite. Conversational analytics module. Not a contact centre QA tool — broader XM platform.
- **Legal SMB relevance:** None. Wrong category, wrong price point.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

## Tier 2: Mid-Market Pure-Play QA (closer to Insights360 but still above SMB floor)

### EvaluAgent
- **URL:** https://www.evaluagent.com
- **Pricing:** From £20/user/month (Auto-QA & Improvement); from £40/user/month (Conversation Intelligence). Annual contract norms.
- **Positioning:** "The #1 AutoQA Platform for Contact Centres." 50–500 agents is their sweet spot. UK-headquartered. G2 Best UK Sellers 2025 and 2026.
- **Features:** AutoQA (100% coverage), scorecards, calibration, LMS, gamification, conversation intelligence, sentiment, CRM/telephony integrations.
- **Weakness:** Economic floor ~50 agents. Below 25 seats, per-seat cost hard to justify. Annual contracts. Implementation not self-serve.
- **Key differentiator vs Insights360:** Stronger coaching/LMS features; weaker on price accessibility at the SMB floor.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

### Scorebuddy
- **URL:** https://www.scorebuddyqa.com
- **Pricing:** Request-a-price (three tiers: Foundation, Accelerate, Elite). Capterra lists ~$15/user as a floor, likely outdated. Vendr positions as mid-market. €5M Foresight funding (Nov 2024).
- **Positioning:** "Integrated QA solution for medium and large contact centres." ISO 27001 certified. Compliance and audit prominent.
- **Features:** GenAI Auto Scoring, scorecards, AI assistance, calibration, coaching (Accelerate+), Conversation Analytics (Elite), voice transcription (Elite), BI dashboards, CCaaS integrations.
- **Weakness:** Opaque pricing — no public rates. AI score caps (500/month Foundation, 1,000/month Elite) add cost unpredictability at volume. Complex onboarding tiers.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

## Tier 3: Full CCaaS Platforms (QA is a module, not the product)

### Talkdesk
- **URL:** https://www.talkdesk.com
- **Pricing:** $85/user/month (Digital Essentials) to $225+/user/month (Industry Experience Clouds). QM tools available from Voice Essentials ($105) upward.
- **Positioning:** Mid-size to enterprise CCaaS. Quality management is a module within a full platform replacement.
- **Weakness vs Insights360:** Full platform migration required — cannot be layered onto existing telephony. 10–16x the price. No legal vertical.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

## Tier 4: Call Analytics / Marketing Attribution (different category)

### Infinity (infinity.co)
- **URL:** https://infinity.co
- **Pricing (UK):** ~£199–£299+/month platform fee + per-call charges ($0.15–$0.20/call on US pricing page)
- **Positioning:** Marketing attribution first ("which campaigns drive calls"), QA and compliance second. Call tracking with AI-powered Conversation Analytics layered on top.
- **Features:** Call tracking with DNI, Conversation Analytics (sentiment, agent scorecards, topic detection), compliance monitoring, 100% call recording, Google/Meta/Microsoft ad integrations.
- **Weakness vs Insights360:** Per-call billing unpredictable for high volumes. QA is secondary to marketing use case. No agent performance analytics or coaching workflow. No legal specificity.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

### Mediahawk
- **URL:** https://www.mediahawk.co.uk
- **Pricing:** £199–£299+/month platform (annual discounts ~10%). Per-call charges additional.
- **Positioning:** Marketing attribution and call tracking. Speech analytics present but marketing-oriented.
- **Weakness:** Same category distinction as Infinity — marketing tool, not QA platform.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

### ResponseTap
- **URL:** Not public pricing.
- **Positioning:** Call tracking and marketing attribution. Similar to Mediahawk.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15

## Tier 5: Managed Services / Integrators (not SaaS products)

### Opus Technology
- **URL:** https://www.opustech.co.uk
- **Pricing:** Not published. Consultative/bespoke.
- **Positioning:** UK managed IT and telecoms integrator. Resells and implements QA and compliance recording via third-party vendors (Wordwatch for compliance recording; Calabrio for QM). Not a SaaS product — a services engagement.
- **Weakness vs Insights360:** Requires consultancy engagement. No self-serve. Underlying QA capability depends on which vendor they've deployed. No legal-sector specificity.
- **Last verified:** 2026-05-15
